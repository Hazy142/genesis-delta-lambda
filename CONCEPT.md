# GENESIS-Δ-λ concept

GENESIS-Δ-λ treats long-lived developmental systems as **lineages with explicit canonical ancestry**, rather than as anonymous benchmark runs or software versions.

## Three layers

For each lineage we distinguish:

1. **Physical capacity `C`** — the bounded substrate capacity made available by one or more devices.
2. **Canonical developed state `K(t)`** — the actually developed nodes, relations, resource state, replay/development state and other Authority-owned structure at time `t`.
3. **Dynamics `D(t)`** — transient activity, recurrence and other time-varying modes over the developed state.

Inter-area coupling is represented separately as `Γ`, because bandwidth, latency, topology and transport semantics affect system-wide dynamics without being equivalent to additional substrate bytes.

## Individual development versus lineage evolution

Within one lineage:

```text
birth → experience → plasticity → sleep/replay → migration → continued development
```

This is an ontogenetic trajectory.

Across lineages:

```text
parent lineage(s)
    ↓ explicit reproduction/inheritance contract
new birth manifest
    ↓
new canonical lineage
```

This is the computational analogue explored by the future phylogenetic research track.

The project does not assume that lifetime memories become inheritable rules. Any inheritance mechanism must state exactly which information class may cross the birth boundary and why.

## Identity principle

A lineage is identified by continuity of canonical Authority ancestry, not by a particular GPU, host, process, runtime version or storage device.

Hardware may change while identity remains continuous, provided the migration/recovery contract preserves the accepted canonical lineage relation.
