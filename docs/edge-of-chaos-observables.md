# Edge-of-chaos observables

`Edge of chaos` is a hypothesis label, not a default property of GENESIS.

A future experiment may use the term only after defining operational observables and a preregistered criterion. Candidate measurements include:

- perturbation amplification;
- activity half-life / reverberation half-life;
- integrated activity/work after a standardized impulse;
- active-node or active-edge peak;
- sensitivity to small input/state perturbations;
- recovery after perturbation;
- context retention after input cessation;
- boundedness / absence of runaway amplification;
- diversity and persistence of recurrent modes.

A useful target regime may be neither maximal decay nor maximal recurrence, but a bounded region where perturbations remain informative for longer without uncontrolled growth.

No single metric, visual pattern or analogy is sufficient to establish criticality. Public claims must state the concrete estimator, controls, null model and uncertainty.
