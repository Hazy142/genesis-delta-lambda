# Public ontology

## `C` — substrate capacity
The bounded physical capacity available for canonical substrate state. Aggregate VRAM is only a coarse upper bound; usable capacity excludes runtime, transport, redundancy and other reserved state.

## `K(t)` — canonical developed state
The Authority-owned developmental state actually formed at time `t`: developed nodes, relations, resource state, replay/development state and other canonical structures permitted by the active contract.

## `D(t)` — dynamics
Transient activity over `K(t)`: excitation, recurrence, dissipation, reverberation and other time-varying modes where the relevant runtime exposes them.

## `Γ` — coupling
The inter-area coupling relation. It may include bandwidth, latency, jitter, reliability, topology and transport semantics. `Γ` is not itself extra substrate capacity.

## `H` — history
The accepted canonical ancestry of states and events leading from birth to the current state.

## Genome / developmental prior
A machine-readable birth/development rule set that may constrain how a lineage can develop. It is not the lineage's lifetime memory.

## Phenotype
The observable developed organization produced by birth constraints plus developmental history.

## Inheritance
An explicit contract that may transfer a bounded class of information from parent lineage evidence into a new birth manifest. No inheritance is assumed by analogy alone.
