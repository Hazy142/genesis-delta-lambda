# Claims and non-claims

## What this repository may claim when backed by retained evidence

- A named lineage record existed at a specific public commit.
- A published cryptographic root commits to a private canonical state or event history.
- A public experiment protocol and its sanitized result satisfy their declared gates.
- A lineage migration, recovery, ancestry edge or birth was privately verified when the exported certificate states the verification method and commitment.
- Aggregate structural or dynamical observables changed over a documented interval.

## What this repository does not establish

A passing gate, large topology, persistent state, replay, recurrence, transfer, prediction lift or multi-substrate execution does **not by itself** establish:

- consciousness or sentience;
- AGI or human-level intelligence;
- biological equivalence;
- self-organized criticality or an `edge of chaos` regime;
- semantic understanding;
- literal neuronal, genetic or evolutionary identity with biology.

## Vocabulary rule

Biological language is permitted as an explicit computational analogy. Every experiment that uses terms such as `genome`, `phenotype`, `inheritance`, `evolution`, `brain area`, `wave`, `criticality` or `edge of chaos` should also define its machine-operational quantity.

## Historical integrity

Negative or inconclusive results remain part of the public research record. Corrections are appended as amendments rather than silently replacing the historical claim context.
